## Design Context

### Users
SparkAPI is an operational admin console for a personal or small-team AI API gateway. Users are technical operators who repeatedly manage upstream accounts, proxies, groups, quotas, billing, and gateway health under time pressure. They need fast scanning, bulk actions, clear failure states, and safe handling of credentials.

### Brand Personality
Pragmatic, controlled, trustworthy. The product should feel like a serious operations tool: calm, dense, predictable, and explicit about risk. It should not feel like a marketing site or decorative dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a restrained enterprise admin aesthetic with high information density, strong table ergonomics, clear hierarchy, accessible contrast, and compact controls. Prefer functional surfaces, readable typography, obvious affordances, and status colors used only where they carry meaning. Support both light and dark mode without relying on glow, glassmorphism, gradient-heavy hero styling, or oversized decorative cards.

### Design Principles
1. Optimize repeated admin workflows first: filtering, selection, pagination, bulk actions, import/export, and error recovery should be faster than they are pretty.
2. Treat credentials and exports as sensitive operations: warn clearly, avoid accidental disclosure, and make destructive or security-relevant actions explicit.
3. Keep dense information readable: align tables, preserve stable row height, avoid layout shifts, and prevent long names or tokens from breaking containers.
4. Make state visible: loading, partial success, validation errors, skipped rows, and background work should be understandable without opening logs.
5. Preserve upstream compatibility while making SparkAPI-specific defaults safer and clearer for self-hosted deployment.
